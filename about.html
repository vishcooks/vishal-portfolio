About

Build it, document it, own it

I'm an Analytics Engineer at Smarsh, embedded in the Product organization. I work with Product Managers, Product Strategy, and Engineering leadership to make GTM and product data trustworthy, documented, and genuinely self-serve.

Over 4+ years I've built dbt and Snowflake data models that power $300M+ ARR planning, forecasting, and revenue analytics. I work across the full ELT lifecycle — PySpark & Delta Lake pipelines, Medallion-architecture modeling, and Power BI / Tableau delivery for cross-functional teams.

Recently I've built GenAI-powered Streamlit dashboards on Snowflake for predictive churn and product adoption, using Cortex Analyst and AWS Bedrock to bring insights directly to revenue leadership. I also ship changes through MCP-connected, AI-agent-assisted workflows under team governance guardrails.

How I work

  • 01Documentation-first. Every model ships with tested metric definitions, lineage, and clear handoffs.
  • 02Insight over method. Lead with the decision the data unlocks, not the pipeline behind it.
  • 03AI as a force multiplier. GenAI for SQL generation, docs, and conversational analytics under governance.
  • 04Global-ready. Comprehensive handoffs so projects progress 24/7 without real-time oversight.
